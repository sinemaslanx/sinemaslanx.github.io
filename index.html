Dr Sinem Aslan

Sinem Aslan,

Associate Professor, University of Milan

Associate Academic Member, European Centre for Living Technology, Ca’ Foscari University of Venice

Dr. Sinem Aslan is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at the Department of Historical Studies at the University of Milan in Italy. Her research spans pattern recognition, computer vision, and machine learning for cultural heritage, covering topics such as the visual analysis of ancient coins, the study of artistic techniques in paintings, and the digital restoration of frescoes. Before joining the University of Milan, she held research positions at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice and the University of Milano-Bicocca, and conducted visiting research at Boğaziçi University in Turkey.

Dr. Aslan has contributed to several interdisciplinary projects, including the EU Horizon 2020 RePAIR (Reconstructing the Past: Artificial Intelligence and Robotics meet Cultural Heritage) project, the BRIDGE project and VENEZIA2021 initiative on lagoon water quality estimation. She has also collaborated within the EU Pathfinder MUHAI project. She has had Area Chair roles at BMVC (2024, 2025), IEEE MLSP 2025, and ICASPP 2026; organizing the FAPER workshop series within ICIAP and the CHAOS challenge; and is an Associate editor at the Journal of the Franklin Institute, Elsevier Array, and Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence. She is also a member of the IAPR Technical Committee 19 on Computer Vision for Cultural Heritage Applications. Dr. Aslan holds a BSc in Electronics Engineering from Ankara University (Turkey), and an MSc and PhD in Computer Science from Ege University (Turkey).
